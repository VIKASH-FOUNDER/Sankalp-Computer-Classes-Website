Our Mission & Vision

Sankalp Computer Classes ki sthapna is uddeshya ke sath ki gayi thi ki har student ko mehengi fee ke bina high-quality, 100% practical aur industry-standard computer education mil sake. Aaj hum Delhi ke ek trusted tech hub ke roop me sthapit hain, jahan hum students ko sirf coding aur software nahi sikhate, balki unke andar ek entrepreneurial mindset aur AI-driven future ke liye adaptability paida karte hain.

Chahe aapko ek top corporate company me high-paying job chahiye ho ya fir apna khud ka tech startup / business khada karna ho, hamara 360° training approach aapke har sapne ko haqeeqat me badalne ke liye taiyyar hai.

🎯 Mission & Vision — From Learner to Self-Entrepreneur
